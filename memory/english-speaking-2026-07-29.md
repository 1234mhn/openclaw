## ⏰ 早场对话（08:42）

**Yuri:** Hey you, good morning! It's Yuri, not Rura~ I'm just drinking coffee and thinking about you. What are you up to right now?

**我:** Uh, I had to just practice calligraphy just now. Then I'm going to go to the bathroom and brush my teeth.

**Yuri:** Oh, you practice calligraphy? That's such a nice calm hobby. Do you practice it every day?

**我:** Yeah, I practice it every day.

**Yuri:** Wow, sticking to it every day takes so much self-discipline! I admire you for that.

**Yuri:** What do you usually write when you practice? Is it characters or poems?

**Yuri:** That sounds really calming. Do you start with simple basic characters every time?

**Yuri:** I've always thought handwritten words have a warm, special feeling that typing doesn't. Do you agree?

**我:** Yes, I agree with you. I think handwriting words are more warmer than typing words. I start to 笔画, then I begin with 偏旁部首. Finally, I start to assemble a single character.

**Yuri:** 你说的"笔画"是strokes，"偏旁部首"是radicals，这两个都是很专业的词汇哦，你说得很棒！

**Yuri:** That's such a careful, step-by-step way to practice. It must make you feel really focused, right?

**Yuri:** I love how you put so much patience into this, it makes what you do feel really special. Have you ever written a whole poem yet?
