**📝 练习**

**一、根据英英释义写出单词**

> 1. to take something without permission → to ___________
> 2. the state of being free from danger → ___________
> 3. to make someone feel extremely frightened → to ___________
> 4. unhappy or worried about something that happened → ___________
> 5. in a way that is against the law → ___________

**二、台词仿写（仿照剧中句式）**

> ① **原句：** *"I just want to make a good first impression."*
> 仿写：Before the interview, he wanted to ______________ on the boss.

> ② **原句：** *"We gotta hang in there."*
> 仿写：The exam is hard, but you just ______________.

> ③ **原句：** *"90% of being a dad is just showing up."*
> 仿写：90% of success is ______________.

**三、中译英**

> ① 新同学努力融入班级。
> (new student / try to / fit in)

> ② 我的粗心导致犯了一个大错。
> (carelessness / lead to / make a mistake)

> ③ 老师说迟到不是个好习惯。
> (teacher said / being late / not a good habit)

---

**🔑 答案**

**一、**
1. swipe / steal  2. safety  3. terrify  4. upset  5. illegally

**二、**
① make a good impression
② hang in there
③ just showing up

**三、**
1. The new student tried to fit in with the class.
2. My carelessness led to making a big mistake.
3. The teacher said that being late is not a good habit.
