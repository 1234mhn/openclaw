**📝 练习**

**一、根据英英释义写出单词**

> 1. very noisy and full of energy → ___________ (adj.)
> 2. unable to think clearly, confused → ___________ (adj.)
> 3. to form a wrong opinion about someone → to ___________ (v.)
> 4. the qualities that make someone who they are → ___________ (n.)
> 5. not open to discussion or negotiation → ___________ (adj.)

**二、台词仿写（仿照剧中句式）**

> ① **原句：** *"You still walk on eggshells around him."*
> 仿写：The new boss is so strict that everyone ___________________.

> ② **原句：** *"It's not the end of the world."*
> 仿写：You failed the exam, but ___________________.

> ③ **原句：** *"Not in those exact words, no. Not in any words."*
> 仿写：He never said he loved me, at least ___________________.

**三、中译英**

> ① 他总是对小事斤斤计较，简直荒谬。
> (always / make a big deal / ridiculous)

> ② 我坚持要自己付钱，但他不肯。
> (I insist / pay for myself / wouldn't let me)

> ③ 她没有自己的身份认同，总在模仿姐姐。
> (doesn't have / her own identity / always copies)

---

**🔑 答案**

**一、**
1. boisterous  2. loopy  3. misjudge  4. identity  5. nonnegotiable

**二、**
① walks on eggshells around him
② it's not the end of the world
③ not in those exact words

**三、**
1. He always makes a big deal out of small things, which is ridiculous.
2. I insisted on paying for myself, but he wouldn't let me.
3. She doesn't have her own identity and always copies her sister.
