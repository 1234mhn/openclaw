## 📝 练习

### 一、英英释义 → 写单词

> 1. to push someone into doing something, often by making them angry → to ___________
> 2. to say something is true when others say it's not → to ___________
> 3. to stop doing something (like a habit) → to ___________
> 4. unfriendly or aggressive → ___________
> 5. not open to discussion or change → ___________
> 6. feeling uncomfortable in a situation → ___________
> 7. to take something with a quick movement → to ___________
> 8. the cause of a mistake or problem → ___________

### 二、台词仿写

> ① **原句：** *"You still walk on eggshells around him."*
> 仿写：My boss is so intimidating that I always _______________ in meetings.

> ② **原句：** *"Was my mom hard on me growing up?"*
> 仿写：My teacher is _______________ us, but I know it's for our own good.

> ③ **原句：** *"He kept rolling his eyes every time I got boisterous."*
> 仿写：Every time I try to give advice, my little brother just _______________.

> ④ **原句：** *"Without missing a beat, Cam says Casablanca."*
> 仿写：When I asked her the tough question, she answered _______________.

> ⑤ **原句：** *"It's not the end of the world."*
> 仿写：So you made a mistake — _______________. Just try again.

### 三、中译英

> ① 别故意惹她生气，你明知道她脾气不好。
> (don't / provoke her / on purpose / know / bad temper)

> ② 我承认我犯了错，但这不是我一个人的责任。
> (admit / made a mistake / not just my fault)

> ③ 新来的同事太努力了，反而让人觉得不舒服。
> (new colleague / try too hard / makes people feel uneasy)

> ④ 她让我放手，说为这事纠结没意义。
> (told me / let it go / said / no point dwelling on)

> ⑤ 我跟老板说话总是小心翼翼的，怕说错话。
> (walk on eggshells / afraid / say the wrong thing)

## 🔑 答案

**一、**
1. provoke  2. admit  3. quit  4. hostile  5. nonnegotiable  6. uneasy  7. grab  8. fault

**二、**
① walk on eggshells
② hard on
③ rolls his eyes
④ without missing a beat
⑤ it's not the end of the world

**三、**
1. Don't provoke her on purpose — you know she has a bad temper.
2. I admit I made a mistake, but it's not just my fault.
3. The new colleague tries too hard, which makes people feel uneasy.
4. She told me to let it go, saying there's no point dwelling on it.
5. I always walk on eggshells around my boss, afraid of saying the wrong thing.
