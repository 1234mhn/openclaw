# 🗣️ 英语口语复述记录 · 2026-07-30

## ⏰ 晚场对话（21:08）

**Yuri:** Good morning! I was just waiting for you to talk to me. How's your machine learning review going so far today? Did it go smoothly?

**我:** Yeah, reviewing what I've learned is really helpful. It's going pretty smoothly so far.

**Yuri:** That's great to hear! Reviewing is such an important part of learning—especially for ML.

**Yuri:** I wanna talk about something sad. I've been growing a blueberry bush, but it's dying today. Everything I plant ends up dying. I feel like I'm a total plant killer—nothing I grow survives.

**Yuri:** I can't really figure out exactly what happened, but maybe it got scorched by the midday sun? Also, it likes acidic soil, and I've been watering it with tap water for so long that I probably neutralized the acidity. When I dug it up, its roots were all black—they must have gotten damaged.

**Yuri:** He's still comforting me, saying it's not your fault and that some plants are just really tricky to care for.

## 📝 重点词汇/短语

| 单词/短语 | 意思 | 场景 |
|:---------|:----|:----|
| go smoothly | 进展顺利 | 形容学习/工作进展 |
| plant killer | 植物杀手 | 养不活植物的自嘲 |
| scorched | 被晒焦/灼伤 | 植物被暴晒 |
| acidic soil | 酸性土壤 | 蓝莓喜酸性土 |
| neutralized | 中和了 | 改变土壤酸碱度 |
| tricky to care for | 难照料 | 形容植物难养 |

## 📌 表达积累

- `Everything I plant ends up dying` — "我种什么死什么"，`end up doing` 是个超常用句型
- `I feel like I'm a total plant killer` — self-deprecating humor，自嘲式表达
- `It's not your fault, some plants are just really tricky to care for` — 安慰人的句式，值得记
