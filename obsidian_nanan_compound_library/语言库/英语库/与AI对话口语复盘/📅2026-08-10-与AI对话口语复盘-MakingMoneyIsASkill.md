# 📅 2026-08-10 · 与AI对话口语复盘

> **场景标签：** `💡个人成长` `💰赚钱思维` `🎓教育观念` `🗣️观点表达`

---

## 句子① — 总起：我想聊聊怎么赚钱

> **原句：** I want to talk about how to make money. I've been thinking about how to earn money on my own.
> **中文：** 我想聊聊怎么赚钱。我一直在自己想这个问题。

---

### 📍 单词

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|
| make money | 赚钱 |
| earn | 挣、赚 |
| on my own | 独自地、靠自己 |
| think about | 思考 |

### 💡 多种表达

- **make money** → earn money / bring in money / make a living（谋生）
- **on my own** → by myself / alone / independently
- **think about** → ponder / reflect on / mull over

### ⚠️ 中式避雷

❌ ~~I've been thinking alone.~~
✅ **I've been thinking about this on my own.**
> "alone" 侧重"一人、孤独"，"on my own" 更自然表达"独自思考/靠自己"。说"一个人在琢磨"用 on my own。

❌ ~~I want talk about…~~
✅ **I want to talk about…**
> 情态动词/动词后接动词要加 `to`：want **to** talk。

### 🔧 万能句型

> **I've been [V-ing]…**
> 我（一直在）做……（现在完成进行时，强调"从过去一直持续到现在"）
>
> 例：I've been thinking about changing my career. / I've been learning Korean this year.

> **I want to talk about [topic].**
> 我想聊聊……（引入话题的常用开场）
>
> 例：I want to talk about my new plan. / I want to talk about what I read today.

## 句子② — 旧观念 vs 新认知

> **原句：** I used to think that if you get into a good school and take things step by step, you can get a good job. But now, I think making money has nothing to do with your education.
> **中文：** 我以前觉得，进好学校、一步步来，就能找到好工作。但现在我觉得，赚钱跟学历没什么关系。

---

### 📍 单词

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|
| used to | 过去常常、以前曾 |
| get into | 进入、考进 |
| step by step | 一步步地 |
| have nothing to do with | 与……无关 |
| education | 教育、学历 |

### 💡 多种表达

- **used to think** → I once believed / I previously thought / I had this idea that
- **step by step** → one step at a time / gradually / little by little
- **have nothing to do with** → be irrelevant to / have no connection with

### ⚠️ 中式避雷

❌ ~~I think making money has not related to education.~~
✅ **I think making money has nothing to do with your education.**
> "与……无关"固定搭配是 **have nothing to do with**，不是 "not related to"（或可说 be not related to）。

❌ ~~I used to thinking…~~
✅ **I used to think…**
> used to 后面接**动词原形**（used to **think**），不接 -ing。

### 🔧 万能句型

> **I used to think [A], but now I think [B].**
> 我以前觉得 A，但现在觉得 B。（表达观念转变，超好用）
>
> 例：I used to think money was evil, but now I think it's just a tool. / I used to think studying hard was enough, but now I know I need to take action.

> **[A] has nothing to do with [B].**
> 和……没关系（强调两者不相干）
>
> 例：Luck has nothing to do with it—it's all about skill. / Talent has nothing to do with age.

## 句子③ — 读书的启发：赚钱是一种技能

> **原句：** I've read a book about this. The author said making money isn't a matter of luck—it's a skill. Society can train you to do a job, but it can also train someone else to replace you.
> **中文：** 我读过一本相关的书。作者说赚钱不是靠运气，而是一种技能。社会能把你培训成一个岗位的人才，同样也能培训别人来取代你。

---

### 📍 单词

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|
| a matter of luck | 靠运气的事 |
| skill | 技能、本领 |
| society | 社会 |
| train | 培训、训练 |
| replace | 取代、替换 |

### 💡 多种表达

- **a matter of luck** → dependent on luck / boils down to luck / just luck
- **it's a skill** → it's a learnable craft / it can be practiced like a skill
- **replace you** → take your place / stand in for you / fill your position

### ⚠️ 中式避雷

❌ ~~I've read a novel about this.~~
✅ **I've read a book about this.**
> 方法论/非虚构书用 **book**，不是 novel（小说）。除非真是小说。

❌ ~~Making money isn't easy, it's a skill.~~
✅ **Making money isn't a matter of luck—it's a skill.**
> 前后对比更有力：不是"容易/难"，而是"靠运气/靠技能"这种对应关系。

❌ ~~train you to replace…~~
✅ **train someone else to replace you.**
> 想说"培训别人来取代你"，用 train sb. to do；意思是"把你替换掉"。

### 🔧 万能句型

> **[A] isn't a matter of [B]—it's [C].**
> 不是靠……，而是……（对比强调)
>
> 例：Success isn't a matter of luck—it's a matter of preparation. / Making friends isn't a matter of money—it's about sincerity.

> **Society can train you to [do A], but it can also [do B].**
> 社会能……，但也能……（指出可替代性）
>
> 例：Society can teach you a trade, but it can't teach you to stand out. / The system can train you for a role, but it can't make you irreplaceable.

## 句子④ — 结论：找到别人无法替代的独特能力

> **原句：** I think what he means is that you need to find your unique edge—something no one else can do as well as you can.
> **中文：** 我觉得他的意思是，你得找到自己的独特优势——一件别人没法像你做的一样好的事。

---

### 📍 单词

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|
| unique edge | 独特优势、过人之处 |
| mean | 意味着、意思是 |
| no one else | 没有其他人 |
| as well as | 和……一样好 |

### 💡 多种表达

- **unique edge** → special strength / something you're best at / a one-of-a-kind advantage
- **what he means** → his point is / what he's getting at / the takeaway is
- **as well as you can** → as good as you / to your level

### ⚠️ 中式避雷

❌ ~~find a unique ability that's specific to you.~~
✅ **find your unique edge—something only you can do well.**
> "unique edge" 比 "unique ability" 更有"竞争优势"的感觉，更贴谈钱的语境。

❌ ~~something no one can do as good as you.~~
✅ **something no one else can do as well as you can.**
> "一样好"用 **as well as**（副词），不是 as good as（形容词）；"别人"用 no one **else**。

### 🔧 万能句型

> **What I mean is that [clause].**
> 我的意思是……（澄清/总结观点）
>
> 例：What I mean is that we should start small. / What he means is that action matters more than planning.

> **You need to find your [unique edge]—something [that…].**
> 你要找到你的独特优势——一件……的事（定语从句补充说明）
>
> 例：You need to find your niche—something the market really needs. / You need to find your strength—something that makes you stand out.

## 🎭 实景对话

> **场景：** 和朋友聊"为什么读书多不等于赚得多"

**A:** I used to think a good degree was the key to a good income. But now I'm not so sure.
**B:** Right? I've read a book that says making money is actually a skill, not a result of education.
**A:** Exactly. And the scariest part is, society can always train someone else to replace you.
**B:** So the real answer is to find your own edge—something only you can do well.
**A:** That's what I'm trying to figure out. What's yours?

## ✍️ 反向仿写

> **中文场景：** 我以前觉得找工作靠学历就行，现在明白了光靠学历不够，得培养别人替代不了的能力。
> **仿写：** I used to think a diploma was enough to land a job. But now I realize a degree alone isn't enough—you have to build a skill that no one else can easily replace.

### 核心句式

| 句式 | 用途 |
|:----|:----|
| **I used to think [A], but now I think [B].** | 表达观念转变（本段灵魂） |

| **[A] isn't a matter of [B]—it's [C].** | 对比强调："不是……而是……" |

### 重点纠错

| 错误 ❌ | 正确 ✅ |
|:-------|:-------|
| thinking alone | thinking about this **on my own** |
| want talk about | want **to** talk about |
| used to thinking | used to **think** |
| has not related to | has **nothing to do with** |
| read a novel | read a **book**（方法论书） |
| isn't easy, it's a skill | isn't **a matter of luck**—it's a skill |
| as good as you | as **well as** you can |
| no one can do | no one **else** can do |
